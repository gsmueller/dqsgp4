## Embedded data values (facts, with their published sources)

Numeric coefficients and physical constants embedded in the source are data from the
published scientific record; each carries an explicit provenance tag and error budget in
code:

- **IAU 2000A luni-solar nutation amplitudes** (678 terms) and the planetary-series
  amplitude totals — data published with the IERS Conventions (2010) / IAU SOFA release
  tables. The SOFA *software* is not used or included; the values are the published
  series coefficients.
- **IAU 2006 precession (Fukushima–Williams) and mean-obliquity polynomials** —
  Capitaine, Wallace & Chapront (2003); IERS Conventions (2010) Ch. 5.
- **Fundamental-argument and GMST/ERA polynomial coefficients** — IERS Conventions
  (2010); Aoki et al. (1982) for the historical GMST of the TEME convention; the
  IAU 2000 ERA defining pair.
- **EGM2008 zonal gravity coefficients** — U.S. National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency
  (NGA) public release (a U.S. Government work).
- **WGS-72 / WGS-84 / GRS-80 defining parameters** — NIMA TR8350.2; Moritz (1980),
  *Geodetic Reference System 1980*; Hoots & Roehrich (1980) for the WGS-72 SGP4 set.
- **Solar (§25) and lunar (§47, Tables 47.A/47.B) series amplitudes** — Meeus,
  *Astronomical Algorithms* (2nd ed.), themselves derived from VSOP87 / ELP-2000. The
  machine-readable source used at table-generation time was the
  [PyMeeus](https://github.com/architest/pymeeus) project (LGPL-3.0); only the published
  coefficient *values* (data) are embedded, regenerated by `tools/gen_lunar_terms.py`.
- **Piecewise-exponential atmosphere table** — Vallado, *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics
  and Applications*, Table 8-4 (USSA76/CIRA-72-derived published values).
- **RKF7(8) Butcher tableau rationals** — Fehlberg (1968), NASA TR R-287.
- **Defining physical constants** (astronomical unit, nominal solar luminosity, speed of
  light, defining rotation constants) — IAU 2012/2015 resolutions and IERS numerical
  standards, exact by convention.

## Algorithms implemented from the published literature

The SGP4/SDP4 analytical theory (Hoots & Roehrich 1980, *Spacetrack Report #3*; Vallado
et al. 2006), the Cunningham/Montenbruck–Gill geopotential recursion, Battin's
cancellation-free third-body form, the Brouwer/Kaula perturbation forms, and Meeus's
ephemeris methods are implementations **derived from the published theory** (the
repository's derivation notes in `design/derivations/` document each derivation); they
are not copies of any reference implementation.

## Development-time references (NOT redistributed)

The test suite was calibrated against independent references that are **not included in
this distribution**: the JPL DE430 planetary/lunar ephemeris (courtesy NASA/JPL),
ERFA/astropy (the IAU SOFA-derived library), the official SGP4 verification outputs
above, the [python-sgp4](https://pypi.org/project/sgp4/) package (MIT; two optional
cross-check scripts use it if installed), and the CelesTrak *Fundamentals of
Astrodynamics* reference software collection (D. Vallado, **AGPL-3.0**). No source code
from the CelesTrak/Vallado collection is included in this repository; an NRLMSISE-00
atmosphere integration derived from it is therefore **not part of this public
distribution**. The `DensityModel` interface in `src/forces/drag.h` is the supported way
to plug a space-weather density model.
